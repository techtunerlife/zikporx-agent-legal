Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026  ·  App: Zikporx Agent (iOS)
Summary: Zikporx Agent is a self-hosted infrastructure management tool. Your infrastructure credentials and operational data stay on your own Hub server and iOS device. We do not collect, sell, or have access to your data.
1. What We Are

Zikporx Agent is a proprietary iOS application that communicates with a self-hosted Hub server (the "Hub") you operate on your own infrastructure.

There is no central Zikporx cloud service that processes your infrastructure data. You run the Hub; you own the data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The iOS app stores the following locally, in the iOS Keychain:

  • Hub server URL and TLS certificate fingerprint
  • Session bearer token obtained after pairing
  • No infrastructure credentials are stored on the device

This data is protected by iOS Keychain encryption and, where enabled, biometric authentication. It is never transmitted to any third party.

3. Data on Your Hub Server

The Hub server you self-host stores:

  • Operational metrics and resource state for your infrastructure
  • An append-only audit log of all actions taken
  • A knowledge store used by the expert/AI system (local only)
  • Proxmox and other integration credentials you configure

All of this data resides exclusively on the server you control. We have no access to it.

4. Optional Cloud Relay

If you enable push notifications, an optional Zikporx cloud relay maps an opaque installation identifier to your Apple Push Notification (APNs) token. This relay:

  • Handles only opaque IDs and push tokens — no infrastructure data
  • Never receives credentials, metrics, hostnames, or operational details
  • Is entirely optional; the app functions fully without it
5. No Analytics or Tracking

Zikporx Agent does not include any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking. We do not track how you use the app.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple's system frameworks (SwiftUI, WidgetKit, App Intents, Keychain). These are governed by Apple's own privacy policy. No other third-party services are included.

7. Children's Privacy

Zikporx Agent is a professional infrastructure management tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time. The updated policy will be posted at the same URL. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@techtunerlife.com.
